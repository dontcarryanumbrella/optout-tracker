# Data license: site list and opt-out instructions

The `SITES` and `LEFT_OUT` data in `index.html` cover each data broker's name, search and opt-out links, what it asks for, and its instructions and notes. They're adapted from:

> **BADBOOL: the Big-Ass Data Broker Opt-Out List**
> by Yael Grauer and contributors
> https://github.com/yaelwrites/Big-Ass-Data-Broker-Opt-Out-List
> September 2026 update

## Changes made

Some sites from the original list are left out (see `LEFT_OUT`, with the reason for each). Instructions, what to have ready, contact addresses and promised response times were rewritten and checked against each broker's own privacy policy or California's data broker registry.
